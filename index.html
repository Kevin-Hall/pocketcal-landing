Pocket Cal is an all-in-one app that brings together your calendar, to-do list, and tasks into a single timeline

~ what this is ~

A calendar that understands you. Snap a photo of a schedule, write naturally, or just speak. AI translates your thoughts into organized time. No complex menus. No rigid forms.

~ how it works ~

Natural language

Type or speak the way you think. "Meeting with Sarah tomorrow at 3pm" becomes a calendar event. No forms to fill out.

Image recognition

Snap a photo of any schedule, poster, or handwritten note. AI extracts every detail and adds it to your calendar automatically.

Everything in one place

Events and tasks live together. Switch between day, month, and year views. See your entire life at a glance.

Privacy first

All your data stays on your device. No accounts needed. No tracking. Your time is yours alone.

Why this exists

Most calendar apps feel like work. They ask you to adapt to their structure, their way of thinking. We wanted to build something different.

Pocket Cal adapts to you. It understands natural language, reads images, and organizes your time without asking you to change how you think. It's a calendar that feels invisible until you need it.

Time is the most precious thing we have. Managing it shouldn't feel like another task.
