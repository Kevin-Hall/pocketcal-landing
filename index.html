How it works

Built for the way you think

No complex menus. No rigid forms. Just natural interaction with your calendar.

Natural language

Type or speak the way you think. "Meeting with Sarah tomorrow at 3pm" becomes a calendar event instantly.

Image recognition

Snap a photo of any schedule, poster, or handwritten note. AI extracts every detail automatically.

Unified timeline

Events and tasks live together. Switch between day, month, and year views seamlessly.

Privacy first

All your data stays on your device. No accounts needed. No tracking. Your time is yours alone.

Philosophy

Why this exists

Most calendar apps feel like work. They ask you to adapt to their structure, their way of thinking. We wanted to build something different.

Pocket Cal adapts to you. It understands natural language, reads images, and organizes your time without asking you to change how you think.

Time is the most precious thing we have. Managing it shouldn't feel like another task.
