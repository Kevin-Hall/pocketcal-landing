Fin & Fern Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email hello@pocketcal.app with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Moving to a new phone

Open Settings in the game and tap Save a backup, then send the file to yourself. On the new phone, tap Load a backup and pick the file. iCloud Backup and computer backups also carry your progress over automatically.

Restoring the full game

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. If you use Family Sharing, family members can restore it too.

No sound

Check the silent switch. Fin & Fern stays quiet in silent mode and never stops your own music. Ambient sound and music can be turned on in Settings.

Battery

Battery saver in Settings is on by default. The game runs smoothly while you touch it and slows down gently while you only watch. It stops completely when you leave the app.

Lost progress

Your game saves itself every few seconds and every time you leave the app. If something still goes wrong, email us and we will help.
