Micro Ball

A one finger arcade game for iPhone. Hold to slow time, drag to aim, release to keep one tiny ball in play.

Free to play

Every mode is free. Videos are always your choice: watch one to revive a run or to double the coins you earned. Remove Ads is a one time purchase that turns the videos off, lets you revive for 25 coins and adds a 25% coin bonus. Coin packs are optional. No subscriptions.
