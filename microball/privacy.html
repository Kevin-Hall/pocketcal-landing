Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Micro Ball is a game for iPhone made by Kevin Hall. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your scores, coins, unlocks and settings stay on your device.
  • Micro Ball does not ask to track you across other apps and websites.
  • Three outside services help run the game: Google AdMob for the optional videos, RevenueCat for purchases, and Aptabase for anonymous usage stats. Each is explained below.
  • We do not sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Optional videos (Google AdMob)

Micro Ball has no banners and no forced ads. A video plays only when you choose to watch one, to revive a run or to double your coins. The Remove Ads purchase turns the videos off entirely.

The videos come from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK starts when the app opens so a video is ready if you ask for one. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, operating system, IP address (which gives an approximate location), how you interact with ads, diagnostic information, and an app identifier for your device. Micro Ball does not ask for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not shared with Google.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Coin packs and Remove Ads are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and to restore Remove Ads on a new device. RevenueCat gives the app a random, anonymous ID and receives the App Store transaction for that ID, along with basic technical details such as the app version, device type, operating system, country or region, and the IP address the request comes from. It does not receive your name, email or Apple Account.

Anonymous usage stats (Aptabase)

To learn how the game is played, Micro Ball sends anonymous stats through Aptabase. A stat is an event name, such as a game starting, a game ending with its score or the store opening, plus a few small numbers and the app version, operating system version, device model and language. Stats contain no device identifiers and no names, and they cannot be linked to you.

How we use information

Only to run the game: showing the videos you ask for, delivering and restoring what you buy, and improving Micro Ball. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to identify you.

Your choices

  • You never have to watch a video. They only play when you tap to watch one.
  • You can limit ad personalization on your iPhone in Settings, under Privacy & Security.
  • You can delete the game at any time to remove everything it saved on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting Micro Ball removes your save from your device. Because there are no accounts, we hold nothing that ties back to you, but you can email us and we will help with any request about the anonymous purchase record kept by RevenueCat.

Children

Micro Ball is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
