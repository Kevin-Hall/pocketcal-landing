Micro Ball Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email hello@pocketcal.app with your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

Touch and hold anywhere on the lower half of the screen. Time slows down while you hold. Drag to aim, then release to send the ball back over the net. Land between the eyes of the platform for a perfect, and fill the five dots to start a fever for double points.

Restoring Remove Ads

Open Settings, tap Store, then tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. You can also reach the Store from the Shop by tapping your coin balance.

Coins and unlocks

Coins, unlocked items and best scores are saved on your device. Coin packs are used up as you spend them, so Restore purchases brings back Remove Ads but not coins. Deleting the game deletes your coins and unlocks.

A purchase did not arrive

Close the game fully and open it again while online. If the coins or Remove Ads are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

Videos

Micro Ball never interrupts you with an ad. A video only plays when you tap to revive a run or to double your coins. If no video is available at that moment, those buttons do not appear.

Sound, haptics and motion

Open Settings to switch sound and haptics on or off, and to turn on Reduce motion for calmer effects.
