Support

Questions, a typo in the text, or an idea? Write any time and you will hear back from the person who makes the app.

Email hello@pocketcal.app

Restore Sacred Heart Plus

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Plus with.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sacred Heart Plus. Reading the whole Bible stays free either way.

Notes on another device

Turn on iCloud for Sacred Heart Bible in the Settings app (your name, iCloud, Apps Using iCloud). Your highlights and notes then follow you to every device signed in to the same account.

Daily Gospel reminder

Turn it on, or change its time, in the app's Settings under Daily Gospel. If it does not arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Sacred Heart in the Settings app.

A more natural reading voice

In the Settings app, open Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English, and download a voice marked Premium. Listen uses it next time.
