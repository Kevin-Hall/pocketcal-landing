Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Sacred Heart Bible is a Catholic Bible for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Sacred Heart Bible does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Your highlights and notes

Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading progress and prayer journal are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they are also kept in your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Sacred Heart Plus is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple only tells the app whether Plus is active, so it can open its features.

Photo search

When you search for a background photo for a verse card or widget, the words you type are sent to Unsplash to find photos. Nothing that identifies you is sent. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to that request.

Reminders

Daily Gospel reminders, and the reminder before a free trial ends, are scheduled on your device. No information leaves your device to send them.

Children

Because Sacred Heart Bible collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
